WHAT WORKED

Pacing and adequate capacity made parallelism useful.

The compact test completed all records; the 120 RPM client cap let five workers finish sooner. The separate large-input experiment admitted all six requests after increasing allocation.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH

A simple task is not a complex prompt.

V1 used a short three-field classification task. Large-input variants added reference text, not the active v2 taxonomy. They measure volume pressure, not the same reasoning complexity.
