The lesson: faster processing, fewer 429s, and correct answers are three different goals.

V2 · GPT-5.4 MINI · COMPLEX TASK

All requests completed. Most analyses still failed strict checks.

The active 425-route prompt produced 39/39 schema-valid outputs across three modes, with no observed 429s. Only 1/13 records per mode passed every gold-field and rule check.

Worked: processing and throttling control. Failed: semantic acceptance, including confidence and cause-extraction rules.
